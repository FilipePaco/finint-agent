## Known limitations

- Synthetic data: no real customers, and names are generated.
- The dataset covers about ten active days; longer histories in reports are simulated and labelled as such.
- 1968 laundering transactions have no typology in the patterns file.
- USD amounts use the simulator's fixed rates, not market rates.
